Project Overview

📊 Apex Cycle Dynamics | Sales & Operations Intelligence

I'm excited to share one of my recent Power BI portfolio projects, where I built an end-to-end Sales & Operations Intelligence solution for a fictional company, Apex Cycle Dynamics, using AdventureWorks data. The goal was not simply to create a visually appealing dashboard, but to transform raw data into meaningful insights that can support data-driven decision-making.

The Project Journey

🔹 The project journey started with raw data:

1️⃣ Data Cleaning & Transformation

I used Power Query to clean and transform the datasets, handle data quality issues, review data types, and prepare the tables for analysis.

2️⃣ Data Modeling

I designed a structured Data Model connecting sales, customers, products, resellers, employees, and territory data while establishing the appropriate relationships between tables.

3️⃣ Calculated Columns & DAX Measures

I created calculated columns and DAX measures to calculate key business metrics such as:

  • Total Sales - aggregating revenue across all channels.
  • Total Orders - tracking volume and order frequency.
  • Average Order Value (AOV) - assessing transaction-level performance.
  • Profit Margin - evaluating financial health per product/category.
  • Active Resellers - monitoring partner network vitality.
  • Sales Growth - calculating period-over-period percentage increases.
  • Customer Metrics - analyzing retention and lifetime value.
  • Employee Performance - evaluating individual sales achievements.

4️⃣ Data Analysis

The analysis covered sales performance across products, categories, territories, and customers, as well as reseller and employee performance and product status.

5️⃣ Dashboard Development

I then transformed the analysis into a multi-page interactive Power BI dashboard covering:

  • 📌 Executive Overview - High-level KPIs and navigation cards.
  • 📌 Sales Overview - Trend lines, regional breakdowns, top products.
  • 📌 Product Analysis - Profit margins, lifecycle status, price tiers.
  • 📌 Reseller Performance - Partner status, top performers, risk levels.
  • 📌 Employees - Department sizes, leave patterns, top salespeople.
  • 📌 Customers & Territory - Geographic distribution and customer segmentation.
  • 📌 Financial Summary - Comprehensive margin and cost summaries.
  • 📌 Call Center - Support metrics and operational efficiency.

The dashboard incorporates Slicers, KPIs, Cards, Charts, Tables, Top-N Analysis, and Interactive Navigation to create a complete analytical experience.

Core Objective & Outcome

🎯 One of the most enjoyable parts of this project was turning raw tables and numbers into a clear data story — starting with data cleaning, understanding relationships, building DAX measures, analyzing the business, and finally presenting the insights through an interactive dashboard.

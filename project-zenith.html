Project Overview

📊 Zenith Retail | Sales & Operations Intelligence

🚀 A project I recently completed with my analytics practice, Insightra: a full Power BI reporting solution for a retail business (Zenith Retail).

The project involved transforming raw sales, customer, product, reseller, employee, and territory data into a structured data model, creating calculated columns and DAX measures, performing in-depth analysis, and finally presenting the insights through a multi-page interactive Power BI dashboard.

The Project Journey

The project journey started with raw data

Data Cleaning & Transformation

I used Power Query to clean and transform the datasets, handle data quality issues, review data types, and prepare the tables for analysis.

Data Modeling

Imported sales, customer, product, and feedback data and connected them with proper relationships (Customer ID, Product ID, Invoice ID), validating cardinality and cross-filter direction across the model.

Calculations

Built calculated columns for revenue, profit, average rating, month name, and a 3-tier rating classification (High / Medium / Low), plus core DAX measures: Total Revenue, Total Profit, and Unique Customers.

  • Built calculated columns for revenue, profit, average rating, month name, and a 3-tier rating classification (High / Medium / Low), plus core DAX measures: Total Revenue, Total Profit, and Unique Customers.

Reporting

Designed an overview page with revenue, profit, and customer satisfaction, a revenue breakdown by category and product, and a monthly sales trend — all wired together with report interactions so filtering one visual updates the rest.

AI-powered analysis

Used Key Influencers to identify what actually drives high customer ratings (and solved a tricky issue along the way by flattening a multi-hop relationship into a single query for the model to analyze correctly), plus Decomposition Tree and Q&A for ad-hoc exploration.

Leveraged Power BI's AI capabilities to generate insights, identify trends, and highlight anomalies in sales and customer behavior.

Interactivity

Added bookmarks and custom navigation buttons, field parameters to toggle the analysis axis between region and category, and custom tooltips for extra context on hover.

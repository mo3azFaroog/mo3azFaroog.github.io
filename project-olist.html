Olist E-Commerce Analytics

From raw CSV files to a star-schema warehouse and an interactive Power BI dashboard

Executive Summary

Project Overview

📊 Olist E-Commerce Analytics | From Raw Data to Business Insights

An end-to-end analytics project built on the public Brazilian Olist e-commerce dataset (9 CSV files, 2016–2018). Instead of stopping at charts, I built the full pipeline: staging the raw data in SQL Server, loading it through Informatica ETL into a star-schema data warehouse, and presenting the results in a multi-page interactive Power BI dashboard.

The dashboard answers key questions: how much revenue, orders, freight cost and customers the marketplace generates, which states and cities drive sales, which products and sellers perform best, and how revenue changes over time.

The Project Journey

Raw Data & Staging

Imported the 9 Olist CSV files into a SQL Server staging schema (OlistDB.stg) and profiled the data to understand its structure, keys and quality issues before any transformation.

ETL with Informatica

Used Informatica to clean, transform and load the staged data into the data warehouse tables.

Data Warehouse (Star Schema)

Designed a dimensional model with one fact table (FactSales) and four dimensions: DimCustomer, DimSeller, DimProduct and DimDate. Surrogate keys keep relationships simple and filters clean.

Data Modeling in Power BI

Connected the warehouse tables with one-to-many relationships, built a calendar table marked as a date table, and sorted months and quarters by their numeric columns.

DAX Measures

Built all measures in a dedicated _Measures table: Total Revenue, Total Orders, Total Freight, Total Customers, Average Order Value, Revenue YTD, Revenue LY, Revenue YoY %, month-over-month comparisons, Best Month, plus repeat-customer and per-seller metrics. Colour and text measures drive the KPI change indicators.

Reporting

Designed a dark-themed, five-page dashboard with consistent headers, home-button navigation and slicers for date, category and state.

Dashboard Pages

Executive Summary

Headline KPIs with month-over-month indicators, revenue and orders trend, top 5 cities by revenue and top 5 product categories by orders.

Sales Map

Total orders by state and city across Brazil.

Seller Performance

Total sellers, revenue and orders per seller, top 10 sellers, top 10 cities by revenue, and a scatter of orders versus revenue per seller.

Customers

Customer counts, repeat-customer rate, orders and revenue per customer, customers by city and a city-level table.

Time Analysis

Revenue YTD, year-over-year growth, best month, revenue versus last year by month, revenue by quarter and a year-by-month matrix.

Key Results

13.59M total revenue • 99K orders • 96K customers • 2.25M freight cost • ~3K sellers.

Revenue grows sharply from 2017 into 2018, and November 2017 is the strongest month, consistent with Black Friday demand. Sales are concentrated in a few cities, led by São Paulo, and most customers order only once (repeat-customer rate is about 3%).

Tools Used

SQL Server • SSMS • Informatica • Dimensional Modeling (Star Schema) • Power BI • DAX

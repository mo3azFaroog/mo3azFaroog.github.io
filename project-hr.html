Project Overview

📊 Salary Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive Power BI dashboard that gives a comprehensive view of salary data, including how salaries are distributed and how they differ across job titles, industries and company sizes.

The Project Journey

Understanding the data

The dataset has 10 core columns: Job Title, Experience Years, Education Level, Skills Count, Industry, Company Size, Location, Remote Work, Certifications and Salary.

1. Data Cleaning & Preparation

I cleaned and prepared the data in Power Query, standardizing columns and handling missing values.

2. Calculated Columns

I built calculated columns such as an experience-level classification and Skills Density (the number of skills relative to years of experience).

3. DAX Measures

I wrote DAX measures for the averages and counts used across the report: Average Salary, Median Salary, Employee Count and more.

4. Dashboard Design

I designed the dashboard across 4 pages:

  • Overview
  • Salary Insights - education, experience, industry and work mode
  • Company & Location Analysis - company size, countries and certifications
  • Location & Work Arrangement - geographic distribution and work mode

Key Insights

  • ✅ Experience raises salary, but the spread widens with more years. Other factors, such as industry and skills, play a bigger role over time.
  • ✅ Higher education (PhD/Master) clearly raises the average salary.
  • ✅ Professional certifications are directly linked to higher pay. The more certifications, the higher the salary.
  • ✅ Company size matters a lot. Large (Enterprise) companies pay far more than small ones.

Tools Used

  • 🛠️ Power BI - For data modeling, DAX calculations, and dashboard design.
  • 🛠️ Power Query - For data cleaning and transformation.
  • 🛠️ DAX - For creating calculated columns and measures.
  • 🛠️ SQL - For querying and extracting data from databases.

Core Objective & Outcome

🎯 An excellent exercise in analytical thinking and in designing a dashboard that communicates information clearly and looks visually organized.

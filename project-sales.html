Project Overview

📊 Sales Data Analysis | Sales & Operations Intelligence

I've been growing my data analysis skills lately, and I'm excited to share a project I really enjoyed building — an interactive Power BI dashboard that turns three years of sales data into a clear, easy-to-read story.

A quick look at the numbers:

The dashboard tracks $24.01M in total sales across 25K orders and 18K customers, with an average sale value of $1.14K. Here's how sales grew year over year:

  • 2020: $6.40M
  • 2021: $8.93M
  • 2022: $8.68M

What the dashboard covers:

Summary Overview

key KPIs at a glance, with a region filter and a highlight showing Australia as the top-performing region

Sales Overview

a breakdown by product model (the Mountain-200 leads the pack at ~30% of total sales) and by region (Australia at $7.2M, followed by Southwest at $4.6M), plus monthly sales trends across the year .

Interactive Map

total sales visualized geographically, with strong performance across Australia, Europe, and North America

Along the way, I got hands-on practice with:

Power Query

cleaning, unpivoting, and combining multiple sales tables into one clean dataset.

Data Modeling

connecting Sales, Product, Customer, and Territory tables.

DAX Calculations

writing measures for key business metrics.

Data Visualization

building an interactive dashboard with slicers, cards, charts, and maps.

This was a great way to practice turning raw data into something genuinely useful for decision-making — and I'm looking forward to applying these skills further.
